Northern Tian Shan

Alpine landscapes, mountain lakes and nomadic culture on one private route from Almaty to Bishkek.

Overview

Two countries, one mountain range

The northern ridges of the Tian Shan run from Almaty to Lake Issyk-Kul. This route follows them through canyons, spruce forests and high pastures, crossing into Kyrgyzstan on the way. You travel by 4×4 with a driver-guide, stop wherever you like, and sleep in hotels, guesthouses and a yurt camp.

Most days include an optional hike. Drives last two to five hours, broken by picnic and photo stops.

Big Almaty Lake at 2 511 m and the Shymbulak cable car
Charyn Canyon’s Valley of Castles on foot
Kolsai and Kaindy, the sunken forest lake
Shyrdak felt workshop with a Kyrgyz family
A night in a yurt camp on Issyk-Kul’s south shore
Falconry show with a berkutchi near Bokonbaevo
