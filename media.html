Media & influencer trips

Tell the world about Central Asia

Familiarisation trips for travel writers, journalists, photographers and content creators.

We believe Central Asia deserves to be better known as a travel destination, and we want to share its landscapes, heritage and people with a wider audience.

If your work focuses on travel, culture or tourism, and your audience is curious about new destinations, we would be glad to consider you for one of our familiarisation trips.

Depending on your profile and the collaboration you propose, we may offer a place on a selected tour at no cost. You see the region first-hand, try our itineraries and form your own view.
