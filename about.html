About us

Experts in private travel across Central Asia

Guides, drivers and trip planners who know the five countries from the inside.

5-Stans is a boutique travel company specialising in tailor-made journeys across Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan and Turkmenistan.

We have no ambition to become a mass-market tour operator. Our priority is a personal approach to every journey.

Our team brings together guides, travellers, drivers and tour planning specialists with over 100 years of combined experience in leading journeys and expeditions across the region.

We introduce guests not only to the sights but to the living culture of Central Asia, through traditional workshops, hands-on crafts and time with local families.
