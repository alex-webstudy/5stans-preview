About Us

Private travel experts in Central Asia

5-Stans is a team of travel experts organising individual journeys across Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan and Turkmenistan.

Our team brings together guides, travellers, drivers and tour planning specialists with over 100 years of combined experience in the region.
