Travel Style & Itineraries

Do you offer group tours?

We specialise in private, tailor-made journeys across Central Asia. You travel with your own party, whether as a couple, a family or a group of friends, without having to accommodate the preferences of strangers.

Travelling privately lets you spend more time at the places that interest you, enjoy a pace that suits you and, within the agreed itinerary, make adjustments along the way.

Can I book a custom tour if I can’t find a suitable itinerary?

Absolutely. Tell us about your interests, dates and preferences. Our Design Your Journey section is made to help you get started.

Can the itinerary be tailored to my interests?

Yes. Private tours adapt to your interests and pace. Our signature tours follow a set concept and sequence; changes to them are discussed separately.

Can I visit several Central Asian countries in a single trip?

Multi-country journeys with several border crossings are one of our specialities. What is possible also depends on your citizenship and current entry rules.

Can I travel solo?

Yes. The per-person cost is higher than for two travellers, as transport and most organisational costs stay the same.

Are your tours suitable for children?

Absolutely. For most itineraries we recommend a minimum age of five, depending on driving distances, altitude and activity level.

What languages are your tours conducted in?

English and Russian are our guides’ main languages. We also regularly guide in Korean and can arrange an interpreter.

Transport & Tour Support

What kind of vehicles will we travel in?

Comfortable 4×4 vehicles, including the Toyota Land Cruiser, Land Cruiser Prado and Toyota Sequoia. Depending on the route we also use saloon cars, minibuses, trains and flights.

What is a driver-guide?

A driver-guide drives the vehicle and introduces you to the geography, culture and nature of the places you visit. For in-depth sightseeing we arrange a separate driver and guide.

Who accompanies multi-day tours across several countries?

A tour leader may coordinate the trip and work with local guides, drivers and hotels in each country.

Are all tours accompanied by a guide?

Yes: a driver-guide, a professional tour guide or a team, depending on the format. The team may change at borders.

How much time will we spend on the road?

Usually two to six hours a day. In Central Asia even short distances can take several hours.

Can we travel with a lot of luggage?

Tell us about large suitcases or sports gear when booking and we will check the vehicle or offer an alternative.

Booking & Pricing

How do I book a trip?

Get in touch with your dates, number of travellers, destinations and preferred style. Sharing flight details helps us plan transfers.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as possible, especially for peak season and remote regions. Early booking means a wider choice of hotels, vehicles and guides.

What is included in the tour price?

It depends on the itinerary. Before you book we send a clear breakdown of what is included and what is paid separately.

Can I get a discount or a special rate?

We keep pricing transparent: we do not inflate prices to leave room for bargaining. Tell us your budget and we can suggest another route or format.

How do I pay for my trip?

Payment terms are agreed at booking, for example in cash on the first day or by another agreed method.

Can I cancel or reschedule my trip?

Yes. Let us know as early as possible; terms depend on the conditions agreed at booking.

Where is your office located?

At the Stanitsa Business Centre in Almaty. Most arrangements are handled online.

Transfers & Arrangements

Can you pick us up at the airport?

Yes, if it is part of your programme or arranged separately. Share your flight number and arrival time.

Will you pick us up from our hotel?

Yes, if included in the itinerary, from an agreed address within our service area.

Where and when will we meet our driver-guide?

At the agreed meeting point, usually 15 minutes before departure. You receive the guide’s name, phone and vehicle details in advance.

What if bad weather prevents us from visiting a place?

Your safety comes first. We assess alternatives and agree the next steps with you.

Accommodation & Meals

Where will we stay during a multi-day tour?

Hotels, guesthouses and family-run lodgings. Facilities in remote mountain areas are simpler; tell us what matters to you.

Can you accommodate vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free diets?

Yes, with advance notice. Alternatives are easier to find in cities than in remote areas.

Is alcohol permitted during the trip?

Moderate drinking by adults is fine where the law allows. Drinks are paid separately.

Preparing for Your Trip

When is the best time to travel to Central Asia?

The main season is April to October. Spring brings blooming valleys, summer suits mountain routes, autumn is great for road trips, and winter shows a snowy side of the region.

What clothing and footwear should I bring?

Shoes with good grip, a waterproof jacket, fleece layers, long trousers and sleeves, a hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, tick repellent and personal medication.

Is the tour suitable for my fitness level?

Our 4×4s reach remote places comfortably, so strenuous activity is rarely needed. Hikes are optional.

Do I need to bring my passport?

Yes. Some routes visit border areas where documents are checked.

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your citizenship. Please check current rules before booking; the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Kazakhstan has up-to-date information.

Do I need travel insurance?

Not mandatory, but strongly recommended, especially for mountain routes and active pursuits.

Comfort, Connectivity & Safety

Will I have internet access during the trip?

Usually in towns and along main roads, not in remote mountains and deserts. Some vehicles carry Starlink. Many apps are blocked in Turkmenistan.

Can I pay by bank card?

In major cities, yes. Carry some cash in local currency for smaller towns.

Is tap water safe to drink?

Check with your hosts and choose bottled water when in doubt. We keep drinking water in the car.

How safe is it to travel in Central Asia?

The countries are generally safe for visitors. Take extra care crossing roads and follow your guide’s advice.

Will medical assistance be available along the route?

Many of our guides hold first-aid certificates. In remote areas medical help can take time to reach.

Are there dress codes I should observe?

Modest clothing at mosques and some rural areas; women may need to cover their heads in mosques and Orthodox churches.

Can I photograph local people and government facilities?

Ask people first. Photography at borders and military or government sites may be restricted, especially in Turkmenistan.

About Us

Who organises the trip, and who will accompany us?

We coordinate transport, route, guiding and local partners. Before the trip you receive details of the people who will travel with you.

Can I read reviews from previous travellers?

Yes, on the platforms where our guests share them, and on our Reviews page.

What makes your trips different from standard sightseeing tours?

A personal approach, careful logistics and Central Asia beyond the usual tourist routes.
